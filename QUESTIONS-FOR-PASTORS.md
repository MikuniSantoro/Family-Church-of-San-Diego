# Questions for Family Church of San Diego

These fill the gaps in the website plan. Where there is a current draft, it is shown in *italics*; answering "keep" is fine. Anything left unanswered stays marked `[CONFIRM]` in the prompts.

## A. Sundays and schedule
1. **How long is the Sunday service, and what is the usual order?** *Draft: about 90 minutes; music and prayer, a message, closing prayer and announcements.*
2. **When does the livestream start**, and is every Sunday service streamed on the YouTube channel?
3. **What language is the service in?** Is there translation (for example Japanese or Korean)? One Christmas photo shows Japanese lyrics on screen.
4. **Picnics:** what time do they usually start, and where are they usually held (the church grounds, or a park such as Lake Murray)? *Rule already set: a picnic Sunday has no service.*
5. **Parking:** is there free parking on site? *Draft: "Free parking on site."*
6. **Refreshments:** is there coffee, tea or food after the service? *Draft: "Coffee, tea and something to eat after the service."*
7. **Children:** is there a nursery, Sunday school or a kids' programme? For which ages, and during which part of the service?
8. **During the week:** are there regular gatherings, with days and times? For example a morning devotion or Hoon Dok Hwe, a Divine Principle or Bible study, a youth group, a young adults group, or choir practice. If there is a devotion on Zoom, what is the link?
