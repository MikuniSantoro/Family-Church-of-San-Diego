A note on giving

Many in our church practise tithing, giving a tenth of what they receive as an offering of thanks to God. Others give what they can, when they can.

Every gift, large or small, is received with gratitude. Please give prayerfully and joyfully, and never from pressure.

If you would like a receipt for your records, email us and we will send one.
